## Pasig archive inspection

[Pasig news archive](https://pasigcity.gov.ph/news-and-releases) uses ordinary `?page=N` pagination via its View More control; category links use `type=news` and `type=event`. No year/search form was observed.

- Pages 112–180 were inspected during the focused older-year search. Pages 127–149 contain 2023; around 149–156 contain late 2022. Normal dated tail entries are September 12–16, 2022. Pages 157–180 and page 200 were empty. Some tail dates are malformed (`0223`, `0024`).
- Titles/excerpts around pages 134–137, covering July–September 2023, and older flood/weather keyword matches were reviewed.
- [July 30, 2023 safety tips](https://pasigcity.gov.ph/news-and-releases/pasig-city-flood-safety-tips-658) contain a rainfall warning and preparedness instructions, not an actual wet-road observation.
- [September 25, 2022 Karding response](https://pasigcity.gov.ph/news-and-releases/tingnan-response-operations-kaugnay-ng-bagyong-kardingph) describes relief packing and checking pumps in preparation.
- [October 29, 2022 Paeng response](https://pasigcity.gov.ph/news-and-releases/tingnan-updating-meeting-at-ilan-sa-response-efforts-ng-pamahalaang-lungsod-ng-pasig-kaugnay-ng-severe-tropical-storm-paengph) describes evacuation, fallen trees and pumping, without individually named wet roads.

This observed listing does not expose normal dated 2021 or January–August 2022 entries. It does not establish that all older posts were deleted or were image only. ### Additional Pasig duration evidence found in city planning material — October 4, 2026

The deeper search located the [Pasig City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Plan 2023–2028](https://www.scribd.com/document/809314092/Pasig-City-DRRM-Plan-9-13-2023), which is hosted as a searchable copy on Scribd. Pasig City’s official [Resolution 271-2023](https://assets.pasigcity.gov.ph/storage/city_resolution/2023/09/11/64feaae064e9e1694411488130-11.pdf) adopted the DRRMC plan, and the [official May 22, 2023 council-meeting post](https://pasigcity.gov.ph/news-and-releases/tingnan-2nd-quarter-pasig-city-disaster-risk-reduction-and-management-council-meeting-328) confirms presentation and approval. The searchable plan describes approximate depth/duration characteristics for Santolan, Ugong, Bagong Ilog, Pinagbuhatan, San Miguel, Caniogan and Santa Lucia. These are community/planning summaries, including historical Ondoy and recurring-rainy-season descriptions, rather than dated flood episodes with measured start and clearance times.

Eight locality-level claims have been transcribed to [`pasig_duration_reference_claims.csv`](pasig_duration_reference_claims.csv) as **context-only reference data**. They are deliberately excluded from `observations.csv`, `candidate_clearance_bounds.csv`, and model training. They can inform later location-aware fallback design and help target data requests, but using them as labels would confuse typical local duration with the outcome of a particular flood.

The expanded web search also re-surfaced a [Manila Bulletin August 31, 2023 report](https://mb.com.ph/2023/8/31/classes-work-suspended-in-ncr-provinces-due-to-heavy-rains-floods) saying minor flooding around Pasig Mega Market soon subsided. Search indexing exposes the relevant passage, but the publisher page remains HTTP 403 in ordinary retrieval. It gives no verified Pasig-specific clock; the 1 p.m. clearance nearby is specifically for Pasay. Retain it as a promising clearance lead, not a captured observation or duration interval, until publisher text/source version and clock can be verified.

## Collection consequence

Pasig 2021 now has more captured evidence, including separate historical occurrence/status records. Pasig 2022 is still sparse and 2023 still lacks a captured specific incident. NCR collection has begun concurrently. The updated graphs are a coverage audit: they identify where acquisition effort and published evidence differ. Neither balanced year bars nor a large raw count is the training criterion; qualified outcomes across independent storms and compatible predictor histories remain necessary.
