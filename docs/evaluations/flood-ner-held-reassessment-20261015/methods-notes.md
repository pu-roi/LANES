# Paper-method notes: laptop source-use research

The dataset remains 400 AI-created, same-agent-reviewed silver location passages from 139 articles, with 200 English/200 Taglish and frozen 280/60/60 partitions. Forty contextual-v3 annotations pair to existing training samples. This milestone performs source-use research, not model training, evaluation, human verification or permission acquisition.

Every one of the 69 held sources has a separate research reassessment with purpose, nature, measured retained/selected amounts, market-effect reasoning, evidence and remaining actions. One narrow proposed private-exception basis covers an AFP-attributed GMA story/three passages; 68 retain the research hold recommendation. All 69 actual project holds remain until a separately supported decision. Seventy other GMA sources/157 passages retain only the delegated adoption reported by the verified desktop summary. No detailed missing desktop decision record was reconstructed and no v2 project-decision checkpoint was created.

All capture dates, raw/text hashes, Unicode clause pointers, partial/failed retrievals and source-specific uncertainty are explicit. Current terms and current attribution may clarify evidence without rewriting original capture dates, identities or body text. The saved complete articles and selected-passage interval unions are different amount measures; previous bounded copy counts are not claimed as a new global cross-device census. A normalized cross-host comparison serves provenance only and never changes annotation text or offsets.

No publisher replies are required as an automatic rule. The statutory and contract analyses remain distinct, no AI-specific blanket exemption or controlling NER case was established by this bounded research, and no court-level fair-use conclusion is asserted. Private-study analysis does not automatically authorize public demonstration, deployed inference, data distribution or model distribution. Do not describe this work as a licensed dataset, 71 admitted articles, an independently reviewed gold corpus or measured NER accuracy.
