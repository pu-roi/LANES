# Paper-method notes: academic-use review

The corrected corpus remains 400 passages from 139 articles, 200 English/200 Taglish with frozen 280/60/60 partitions. Forty contextual v3 training pairs do not add samples. References are AI-created and same-agent reviewed silver labels; no independent human gold verification or model accuracy is claimed.

The project performed three AI publisher-use assessments and evidence/amount reviews for all 139 sources. GMA's policy provides educational/noncommercial downloading support; the complete team NER workflow remains undecided. ABS-CBN and Philstar have no established scope-matched NER permission in the inspected evidence. A case-specific research/fair-use argument is documented as a candidate, not a finding of legal clearance. Publisher website terms and statutory exceptions were considered separately.

Seventy-eight articles have overlapping follow-up flags. These are not counts of infringements or required exclusions. Eight sources are opinion/editorial articles and nine selected passages contain whole captured articles. The reviewer distinguished explicit wire credit, possible wire origin, photo-caption attribution and the non-agency AFP acronym. The selected AFP caption is included in a training passage; no image weights/assets are added.

All three academic-use decisions and all twelve other-use outcomes remain unresolved. Training admission and successful DocBin export remain zero. State actual AI assessor provenance and unknown retention/institutional requirements rather than claiming publisher permission, human legal review or institutional authorization. The intended private research-team/adviser audience is declared; actual access controls and storage/retention arrangements still require documentation.

This review does not modify article text, labels, partitions, tokenizer/model artifacts or the current study package. No model sees news text, no GPU installation or training occurs and nothing is publicly distributed. Software verification and evidence hashes do not establish copyright clearance or NER accuracy.
