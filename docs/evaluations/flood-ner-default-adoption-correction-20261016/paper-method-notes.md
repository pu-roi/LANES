# Method correction: individual source-use assessments

The dataset remains 400 AI silver location records from 139 news articles, paired with 40 contextual records covering the same passages. No model accuracy, independent human annotation verification, publisher permission or admission is established here.

The prior 68-case completion claim is superseded because its builder supplied adoption for 58 cases through a default branch. The revised method reads each complete captured source with every frozen selected interval marked, then records the material's analytical role, factual/expressive character, qualitative importance, selected extent, full-body retention and market limitations. Two exact source-content pointers per article supplement all selected-passage hashes and offsets. Neither pointer presence nor a word-count threshold proves substantive adequacy.

Each of the 58 revised records has explicit four-factor discussion and a dated AI disposition. Shared publisher-policy and contractual analysis remains hash-bound and separate from individual content findings. Acquisition remains Unknown / Codex collected, without a finding of historical assent or non-assent. The ten earlier special-case decision objects and 71 earlier dispositions are preserved. The missing detailed desktop record remains unreconciled.

The applicable statutory framework remains [Section 185 in the consolidated IP Code](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/18399); [IPOPHL explains the four-factor review](https://www.ipophil.gov.ph/news/ipophl-releases-statutory-fair-use-guidelines-to-clarify-rules-on-copyright-exceptions/?news_page=9). Academic purpose does not settle the individual application. A general law/guidance browser check was readable for WIPO/IPOPHL on October 16; the Supreme Court E-Library request failed. This did not replace the existing hash-bound legal snapshots or create new permission evidence. The prior failed direct IPOPHL captures remain historical failures.

Extent includes complete-body retention and known archive representations, not just percentages of selected text. The inherited bounded inventory describes exact body-string occurrences in four unchanged local containers. It is neither a global copy count nor a determination of legally separate reproductions. Current desktop/remote multiplicity and some older-checkpoint copies remain unknown. Multiple archives are not all shown necessary, and the single-private-copy provision is not used to clear them automatically. Retention remains undecided.

Market findings distinguish compact practical inventories, bounded local descriptions and selections taking a report's quotation, policy or human-interest core. Private discriminative study reduces inferred direct reader exposure; it does not measure licensing markets, memorization or cumulative-copy effects. A hold is not an assertion that every quoted official speech is protected: it concerns the selected newspaper reporting/composition and the unresolved need for that exact qualitative extent. Missing bylines, unfavorable terms, agency acronyms or percentages alone do not decide an article.

Derived full-corpus coverage is 125 adopted articles / 332 passages and 14 held articles / 68 passages. Training admission and successful exports remain zero. Resolving the exact holds and reconciling the unavailable predecessor remain subsequent work; neither sample replacement nor publisher correspondence is presumed mandatory.
