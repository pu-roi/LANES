July 25, 2024

FLOOD UPDATE
As of 5:00PM July 25, 2024
Please be advised that Gate 5 Karangalan Village in Brgy. Manggahan is still NOT PASSABLE to light vehicles due to significant flooding in the area.
F. Manalo Bridge, also in Barangay Manggahan, is still closed until further notice.
Please seek alternate route.
For emergencies, you may call Pasig City Hotline at 8643-0000.
Stay safe!
