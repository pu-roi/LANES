# Paper preparation: actual dataset method

> **Last Updated:** October 15, 2026, 2:18 AM by [@roicambe](https://github.com/roicambe) (Roi Cambe)

The dataset comprises 400 contiguous news passages from 139 Philippine news articles published within January 1, 2017–October 14, 2026. It is not 400 articles or 400 training-only observations. The research partition is 280 training, 60 validation and 60 testing passages, with equal English/Taglish counts in each partition. Forty contextual records are paired with existing training passages and do not increase the sample size to 440.

Codex read saved article bodies, proposed source-exact spans and location classes, and reviewed them within the same AI session. Python tooling serialized those decisions into unchanged passage offsets, checked source hashes and tokenizer boundaries, rejected overlaps/copies, applied explicit grouping decisions and solved the fixed selection constraints. Labels are AI-created silver references; no independent human verification, gold labels or inter-annotator agreement was measured. The paper's earlier statement that researchers manually annotated the corpus must be reconciled with this actual method.

Language decisions use an operational lexical-mixing convention: Filipino syntax/content with substantive English words or borrowings is Taglish; named English places/publishers alone do not establish mixing. Pure Tagalog and other Philippine languages were excluded from the selected language scope. This classification has not received independent linguistic validation, and borrowed-word choices can affect language counts.

Location annotations use ROAD, LANDMARK, LOCAL_AREA, BARANGAY, CITY and PROVINCE. Raw spelling is retained. Source-supported parent mentions are separate from resolved PSGC/OSM identity, which remains unknown when unsupported. A named drainage outfall is a landmark in its sentence even when a similarly named street occurs elsewhere. Ambiguous raw municipality spelling is not silently corrected.

The contextual pilot separately records source-supported depths, times, status, vehicle access and relationships. Shared and collective observations are distinguished from uniform point measurements. Local exceptions include narrower spatial observations and later local temporal refinements. Relative phrases and verbal gauges retain uncertainty. Rainfall onset/cessation, mall schedules, project phases and monetary/count references do not establish flood onset, clearance or depth. A missing antecedent leaves a target unknown.

Article versions, copied reports and explicit event dependencies are grouped together. Additional conservative storm-year/place/date links guard against uncertain reporting dependence; they are not confirmed physical incident identities. Fresh validation/testing partitions are frozen before tuning. All ten accepted original pilots are confined to training. The quota policy controls source/label support and publisher concentration, not statistical independence or guaranteed prediction accuracy.

The resulting selection has 1,494 place spans, 45 empty-place records, three publishers and 82 reporting groups. All hard label/publisher/article/group quotas pass. The 40 contextual pairs cover 14 English-source articles and 19 Taglish-source articles and all five required categories. The original 400 dataset and backup remain unchanged; comparison reports describe both revisions rather than presenting the earlier corpus as overwritten.

Software verification reports 525 passing regression tests, source/offset/tokenizer/Unicode checks and deterministic replay. These are tooling results, not model precision, recall, F1 or field validation. Training admission, DocBin export, trained models and measured model accuracy remain zero. Publisher reuse assessments remain unresolved; data are research-only. Full captures are ignored local artifacts and need an external backup. The shared Google document was not edited.
