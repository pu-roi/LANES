# Paper-method notes: acquisition audit and decision drafts

The study remains 400 AI-created, same-agent-reviewed silver location samples from 139 news articles, with 40 contextual-v3 pairs linked to existing passages. The audit creates no additional training samples and measures no model accuracy.

All 68 ABS-CBN/Philstar articles were traced to unchanged browser-rendered snapshots. Exact source text, snapshot hashes and URLs were checked. Snapshot publication headings were distinguished from HTTP request headers. Saved article evidence does not establish the historical login, paid-access, terms-notice or acceptance interactions. Current HTTP collector settings were examined as code evidence only, not substituted for browser history.

Sixty-nine proposed decision records preserve individual four-factor analyses and source-specific questions. One AFP-attributed GMA story has a proposed narrow private-use adoption; all 69 existing project holds remain unchanged. The 70 previously adopted GMA articles are carried forward through the verified desktop summary; the unavailable detailed private checkpoint was not reconstructed. This work does not claim human verification, publisher permission, legal approval, dataset admission, export or training.

All twelve nonacademic-use outcomes are unchanged. No fixed retention period or mandatory institutional approval was invented. The next step is to complete the remaining factual record and reconcile supported project decisions separately; publisher replies remain an optional source of evidence.
