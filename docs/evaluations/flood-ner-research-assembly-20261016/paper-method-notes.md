# Paper-method notes — frozen research assembly v5

The current frozen research revision contains 400 source-exact location samples from 138 Philippine articles published January 1, 2017–October 14, 2026. It has 200 English/200 Taglish samples, split into 280 training, sixty validation and sixty testing records, with 1,465 location spans and 48 passages containing no supported in-profile place. Forty contextual records are paired with those passages and do not increase the sample count.

Assembly froze an already reviewed selection without rerunning an optimizer or changing labels. It preserves 388 location records, including 383 fixed unaffected records, all validation/test samples and ten training pilots. Twelve reviewed replacements and two contextual replacements are introduced, with the other 38 contextual payloads unchanged. Every original publisher, article, reporting-group and per-language label-support quota passes; quotas do not establish representativeness, statistical power or model accuracy.

References remain AI-created silver labels with same-agent review and the existing operational Taglish lexical-mixing convention, including borrowing uncertainty. There is no independent human annotation claim. Original wording, offsets, hashes, source timestamps and unresolved geographic identities are preserved. The tokenizer remains lanes.xx.address-v3 under spaCy 3.7.5; this is not qualification of the calamanCy model tokenizer against news data.

Four scope-specific private academic-use project decisions and 134 preserved dispositions are bound to the new cohort/context manifests and every article/sample membership. The resulting 138-article/400-passage coverage is derived from recorded decisions, partly by desktop-summary reference; the detailed desktop predecessor remains unreconciled. This is neither publisher permission nor legal approval or a public-use license. Source flags remain research_only/unknown, training admission and exports remain zero, and no model was trained or evaluated.

Canonical source-embedding serialization plus one replay produces 960 exact complete-body string fields: 880 embedded source bodies, 24 whole-body passage fields and 56 other evidence fields. This is a bounded representation count, not 960 distinct articles or legal reproduction units. No new source was collected; existing files/archives are unchanged and off-device multiplicity is not fully measured. The complete v2 copy audit supersedes the earlier source/passage-only total of 904.

Thirty-two negative artifact checks, 22 byte-identical replay files and preservation hashes are separate from software/model metrics. The historical 772-passed/one-skipped software result was not rerun. Applying versioned private-use admission, corpus model-tokenizer qualification, training export and measured model training/evaluation remain subsequent work.
