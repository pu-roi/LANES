Flood Update: Severe Tropical Storm Kristine (October 24, 2024)
October 24, 2024
FLOOD UPDATE
October 24, 2024 | as of 11:30PM
Please be advised that the following roads are NOT PASSABLE to light vehicles due to significant flooding in the area:
• Karangalan Phase 2, Brgy. Dela Paz
• Greenwoods Ave. cor. Sandoval Ave., Brgy. Pinagbuhatan
• Pinalad Road (and inner streets - Kaimito St, JP Riza St., and Durian St.), Brgy. Pinagbuhatan
• Market Ave., (vicinity of Mutya ng Pasig Mega Market)
• Urbano Velasco Ave., Brgy. Pinagbuhatan
• Jenny's Ave. cor. Legaspi, Brgy, Maybunga
• Dabba, Paraluman St., Bathaluman St., Brgy. Rosario
• M. Eusebio Ave., Brgy. San Miguel
• Caruncho Ave., Brgy. San Nicolas (in front of Pasig City Hall)
• Eagles St. cor E. Rodriguez Ave., Brgy. Ugong
Please seek alternate route.
For emergencies, you may call the Pasig City Hotline at 8643-0000.
Stay safe!
*This post will be updated as soon as new information from Pasig City DRRMO becomes available.
