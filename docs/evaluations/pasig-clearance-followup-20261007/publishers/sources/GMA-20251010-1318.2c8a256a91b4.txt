Quezon City

State weather bureau PAGASA earlier said the trough of a low pressure area (LPA) is expected to bring cloudy skies with scattered rains and thunderstorms over Metro Manila, Central Luzon, CALABARZON, Bicol Region, and the rest of MIMAROPA.
Cloudy skies with scattered rains and thunderstorms, meanwhile, is seen to prevail over Visayas, Mindanao, and Palawan due to southwesterly windflow.
The localized thunderstorms may cast partly cloudy to cloudy skies with isolated rainshowers or thunderstorms over the rest of Luzon. —VAL, GMA Integrated News
