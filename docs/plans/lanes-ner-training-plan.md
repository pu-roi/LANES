## Gate 1 — research semantics before fixing the annotation design

The developer specifically requested more research. Initial proposed labels and sharing rules are hypotheses until this gate is reviewed; do not train a model merely to reproduce current regex decisions.

### Philippine numbers, locality designators and compound addresses

The developer clarified that 4 and 6 are examples, not the supported number set. Research must cover the number system and source-observed identifiers across locality/address types before implementation or training. The existing parser already contains units, teens, tens, composed tens, hundreds and larger cardinal forms in English, Filipino and Spanish/borrowed spellings. This is an implemented baseline to audit, not proof that every Philippine spelling or address format is supported.

| Research dimension | Required cases and outcome |
|---|---|
| Number coverage | Inspect every digit/unit and teen, each decade and compound tens, representative hundreds and larger identifiers when observed. Include native Filipino, English, Spanish and Filipino adaptations, accents, linkers, joined/hyphenated spellings, and mixed word/digit expressions. Record supported, unsupported and ambiguous forms rather than promising every spelling. |
| Designators and abbreviations | Inventory Sitio, Purok/Prk., Block/Blk., Barangay/Brgy./Bgy., Zone, Phase, Lot, Area, Unit and house/street-number references; research additional regional forms found in sources. Establish evidence for each abbreviation and its role rather than treating all types as interchangeable. |
| Identifier shape | Research leading zeros, letters and suffixes (5A versus 5B), multi-part identifiers, Roman numerals, ordinals, number markers, punctuation and spacing. Preserve the full raw identifier; do not collapse a range, slash code or compound identifier into one simpler place. |
| Combined address | Preserve separate block/lot/phase/unit/street components and their subdivision, sitio/purok, barangay and city context. Determine entity boundaries and parent relationships from source examples; a compound address is not automatically one road or an administrative PSGC entry. |
| Named places and negative cases | Include named/non-numbered sitios and puroks, numbered street names and named developments. Contrast locality numbers with water depths, clocks, counts, dates and ordinary words; name recognition does not justify rewriting a name or inventing a map match. |
| Geographic identity | Compare equivalent forms only within supported type and parent context, retain unsupported/missing PSGC or OSM coverage, and separately score mention recognition, normalization and grounded identity. Repeated numbers in different barangays, cities or subdivisions remain distinct. |

**Observed implementation gaps:** the prefix pattern currently accepts sitio, purok, zone, phase, block, barangay and brgy, but not blk, prk, bgy, lot, area or unit. A local probe left `Blk. 12`, `Prk. Singko`, `Lot 7` and `Phase II` unchanged while recognizing `Purok labing-isa`, `Block treinta y dos`, `Zone siyamnapu at siyam` and `Sitio isang daan at lima`. These are constructed probes, not captured news or a newly completed full evaluation. Existing letter/leading-zero normalization also needs source-backed review before its equivalence is applied to additional address types.

The [Quezon City government address directory](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/departments/social-services-development-department/) provides real examples combining lot/block, phase, area, unit, sitio and purok references, including abbreviated block notation and Roman numerals. Use official/LGU references to research address syntax and public flood articles to research flood-fact attribution; an address directory is not flood evidence. [BFAR's official registry](https://www.bfar.da.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Registered-P.-vannamei-Extensive-Grow-out-Farm-January-31-2026.pdf) is an additional source candidate for purok abbreviations; inspect exact passages before accepting mappings.

**Research deliverable:** a source-linked coverage matrix and explicit admission/ambiguity rules for number forms, designators, compound identifiers and parent relationships. Mark coverage gaps and constructed tests separately; do not close this gate based only on the two earlier examples or the existing regression count. Broader aliases and parser behavior remain pending implementation after the research specification. Owner: [@roicambe](https://github.com/roicambe) (Roi Cambe).

### Flood-fact scope and attribution

| Required case | Research/annotation question |
|---|---|
| Sitio/purok number and spelling variants | Reuse existing cardinal equivalence while preserving raw evidence; does the source support the same parent locality and mapped identity? |
| Same name/number in different parents | Which city/barangay context supports the PSGC/OSM match, and when must identity remain ambiguous? |
| Recognized place without map coverage | Record recognition separately from unresolved geographic placement; do not invent a PSGC code, OSM feature or coordinate. |
| Multiple locations, distinct depths, one time | Which shared heading/update supports the time, while each location retains its own depth? |
| Multiple locations, one depth, distinct times | Does the source explicitly apply that depth to each location, and which time belongs to each claim? |
| Multiple locations, one depth and one time | Preserve the single evidence occurrence and its explicit target set; do not pretend there were independent measurements. |
| Shared statement with a local exception | Does a later qualifier, depth, status, access restriction or clock override only one named location? |
| Collective area depth, range or maximum | Preserve collective scope and uncertainty; do not distribute a range or maximum into exact per-road readings. |
| Coordination and discourse | Compare “and/at,” “while/habang,” “meanwhile/samantala,” city/section headings, captions and quoted source changes. |
| Onset versus observation/report time | “As of” confirms a supported observation, not when flooding began; publication/fetch times never substitute for onset. |
| Recession versus clearance | Receding water can remain flooding; explicit clearance needs location/time matching. Expiry does not establish physical clearance. |

Existing implementation supports scoped road lists and some shared narrative clocks. See [heading regressions](../../backend/tests/test_news_heading_context.py), [temporal regressions](../../backend/tests/test_news_temporal_scope.py) and [narrative regressions](../../backend/tests/test_news_september24_replay.py). [apply_shared_update_times](../../backend/app/services/taglish_extraction_service.py) currently treats “meanwhile” as a boundary; do not simply delete that guard. Research positive same-update usage and negative chronology/source-change usage before choosing replacement behavior. Tests were inspected during planning; no fresh test execution is claimed.

**Deliverable:** a source-linked English/Tagalog/Taglish case matrix, ambiguity policy, annotation boundaries, fact-to-location/time/depth relationships and explicit unknown states. Separate real-source cases from constructed regression examples. The developer's Caruncho/Dr. Sixto sentence is illustrative, not a captured news report or training ground truth. ## Gate 8 — production monitoring and operator handoff

Routine operation must be owned by LANES services and Scheduler, without a chat, external agent or provider key being required to complete eligible news processing. Staff retains exceptional correction/review responsibility under DESIGN.md.

## Gate 9 — dataset/model maintenance and controlled replacement

Version captures, annotation revisions, language/label coverage, split membership, training runs and released artifacts. Retain licensing/provenance and document storage access/retention. A changed article must not silently alter an existing training example or its split identity.

Review recurring missed locations, wrong associations, unknown/ambiguous cases, new publisher formats, language changes and geographic coverage. Compare both model/extractor failures and upstream filtering misses; a high score on admitted articles cannot establish RSS discovery recall. Avoid using confidence as ground truth or forcing every exception into a training label.

Retraining is an explicit offline maintenance action triggered by evidence such as sustained measured degradation, new supported labels/languages/areas or enough corrected examples. It never runs automatically inside RSS collection. New AI-assisted labels remain silver unless independently verified; model-generated predictions are not automatically trusted labels.

For every replacement, freeze data/configuration, compare against the deployed model and current rules, run the full acceptance contract and regression suite, evaluate an untouched or newly constructed event-separated holdout, and record all changes. Preserve an older benchmark for regression tracking but report that repeated selection on it reduces independence. Release the replacement through Gates 6-8 with a versioned rollback bundle; no untested artifact overwrite or silent model update.

**Deliverable:** maintenance schedule/owner, retraining triggers, version/retention policy and replacement checklist. Frequency and dataset growth targets are chosen from the measured pilot rather than a promise that training takes a fixed number of days.
