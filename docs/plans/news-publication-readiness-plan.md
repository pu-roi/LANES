## 1. Storage reuse and required additions

Existing `news_article_versions` and `news_extraction_runs` preserve evidence and extraction identity. Their JSONB results must not become mutable publication records. `NewsArticle.review_state` is an article summary and cannot represent independent road decisions. Feed checkpoints and discovery telemetry are not lifecycle storage.

Reuse `FloodEvent`, `FloodEventLocation`, `FloodAvoidanceZone`, event timeline entries and `AuditLog` for verified operational outcomes. Existing events support multiple roads/barangays/cities and separate section-owned polygons. Do not create a verified event for an unresolved alert: events require peak severity and a verification time. Do not create a news claim as a pending citizen report or label it `manual_seeder`.

**Approved and implemented schema scope: five additive tables.** No existing SQLAlchemy model field, geometry type, report-source enum or extraction record changed for this slice. New models export their relationships to existing tables. News provenance comes from the new links; future automatic news zones can use existing severity/depth overrides and `source_geometry` without creating synthetic FloodReports. Report-only contributor APIs must explicitly include news contributors later.

All timestamps use timezone-aware UTC. All foreign keys below use `ON DELETE RESTRICT`; no destructive source deletion or cascade into operational history. Normalized source identities and relationships belong in columns, while immutable external audit payloads and validated decision snapshots use JSONB.

## 2. Identity, duplicate handling and continuity

Retries reuse the unique immutable run/ordinal source binding and policy evaluation. A policy fingerprint includes gate/prompt versions, provider/model and analytical source checksums. Changed policy produces a new evaluation without modifying old extraction or audit evidence.

Bind a revised article claim to an existing case only when source lineage, qualified place, observed incident time and claim meaning establish continuity. Never bind by ordinal across two runs, title similarity, a nearby point or road name alone. Reordered source claims retain their own immutable identities. Uncertain continuity creates an unpublished review case; it cannot refresh or withdraw another case.

Copied/syndicated stories remain traceable sources and do not count as independent corroboration. Conflicting road conditions, separate observation times, disconnected sections or different vehicle restrictions remain separate claims/zone sections. Proven shared incidents may reuse existing events and zones, with explicit contribution links. Claims covering multiple sections can link multiple zones; this does not authorize filling gaps or spreading one depth across them.

### Operational footprint validation contract

Implemented locally under BUG-110; no real current incident catalog is provisioned. The [verified contract and catalog runbook](../evaluations/phase-36-trusted-footprint-contract.md) documents acceptance and current integration limits.
1. **Geometry type & coordinate system:** Polygon/MultiPolygon inputs require explicit WGS84 SRID 4326; Shapely inputs must also carry actual SRID 4326. Each persisted zone is a PostGIS Polygon component. Linestrings/points and 3D/M coordinates reject.
2. **Topological integrity:** Geometry must be topologically valid (`ST_IsValid` / Shapely `is_valid`), non-empty and at least 1 m² per component. Limit to 25 components and 10,000 coordinates; reject invalid/negligible parts without repair, clipping, dropping or bridging.
3. **Locality containment:** Resolve the reported administrative parent from checksummed assets and require `parent.covers(footprint)` over the complete extent, including holes. Missing named barangay never falls back to the city; absent/invalid boundaries or any component outside reject.
4. **Provenance & metadata:** Approval must come from an operator-approved current incident catalog record or authenticated active/capable staff review bound to exact article/input/claim/incident/observation/locality, source/checksum, SRID, parent/whole/component hashes and boundary revision. A payload source label/checksum alone cannot approve geometry. OSM centerlines and NOAH hazard overlaps provide candidate evidence and placement preview auras, not verified real-time operational perimeters.
5. **Non-bridging of disconnected sections:** If a flood affects multiple disconnected segments along a corridor, each qualified section must be persisted as an independent `FloodAvoidanceZone` sharing the parent `FloodEvent`. Never stitch or convex-hull across unverified gaps.
6. **Fallback behavior:** Where no qualifying operational polygon is available, the claim remains an `active_alert` (`geometry_precision="text_only"`, `geometry_reason="operational_geometry_not_verified"`, `affects_routing=False`). The preview aura remains visible in Needs Review inspection without creating an avoidance closure in Valhalla routing.

## 5. Transaction, support and recovery contract

1. **Implemented evaluation stage:** claim one evaluation under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, commit its lease, then perform the external audit outside the transaction. Bound attempts/time/response size and finalize only under matching live lease ownership. Terminal evaluations are immutable; this stage writes no decisions or operational state. Never hold database locks across network calls.
2. In the implemented short source-alert publication transaction using the immutable completed evaluation, serialize verified incident identity with a transaction-scoped PostgreSQL advisory lock before locating/creating a target, then lock the target case, evaluation and affected zones in deterministic ID order. The lock key comes from supported qualified place/section, observation and source continuity; it is concurrency control, not evidence that incidents match. Recheck duplicates under that lock, including when no event/zone exists yet. Uncertain incident identity requires review. Require a completed immutable evaluation bound to the correct source, immutable source hash, current policy/assets, current time and expected case revision. Pending/retry/failed or stale evaluation cannot authorize a decision; publication must recheck current evidence freshness after audit completion. A stale staff revision returns HTTP 409 with the latest safe detail.
3. Recompute eligibility from the immutable completed evaluation; append the decision and increment case revision without mutating or refinalizing terminal audit history. For a verified activation, create/update the event, locations, zones, links, timeline and audit entry in this same transaction. Alert-only outcomes create no domain event/report/zone. Flush helpers; one outer commit owns success. Roll back every write in that claim's transaction on failure. Different claim transactions may succeed independently and must be reported accurately.
4. Refactor `create_verified_event_with_zone` and `deactivate_zone_and_end_event_if_final` to accept backward-compatible `commit=False` participation; existing callers retain default behavior. Pass automatic actor `None` through service annotations and existing nullable actor columns. Avoid `create_flood_report`, which commits internally, and never invent actor ID 1. Store supported observation time as first report time when creating a news event; ingestion time is not onset.
5. Requests reuse globally unique `request_id`; exact retries return their saved outcome without extending expiry. Reusing a request ID with a different case/action/body returns 409; the persisted immutable snapshot carries the canonical request hash. Before zone creation, lock a matched existing operational owner/support target when verified shared-incident identity exists. Proximity alone does not authorize reuse. Serialize competing claims against the same confirmed target; ambiguous identity requires review instead of duplicate routing polygons.
6. On withdrawal/expiry, remove the case's current support in its new decision. Deactivate only zones with recorded news creation ownership and no remaining eligible news support or applicable independent citizen/manual support. Never delete prior polygons/evidence. Shared zones retain their supported coverage; if partial coverage cannot be safely reconstructed from existing provenance, keep independent sections separate and send the conflict to review. Independent support includes existing operational ownership and approved linked reports under their existing lifecycle, not news-count heuristics.
7. For news-owned zones, effective expiry reflects the remaining eligible contribution lifetimes; extending it requires a newer supported observation or independent eligible contribution, never a retry. Existing citizen/manual lifecycle remains authoritative. End an event only after its final active unexpired zone ends. Public readers and routing exclude expired records even if the expiry worker is delayed. Alerts derive visibility from the latest decision's state and expiry; old active history cannot reappear after a withdrawal.
8. Emit existing API-process SSE invalidation only after commit. Collector/job processes do not share the API's in-memory SSE manager. Use durable reads plus existing 15-second polling/focus/reconnect refresh for correctness; do not claim job broadcast reaches all clients. A worker crash after commit but before notification is recovered by polling, while a crash before commit leaves no partial public state.
